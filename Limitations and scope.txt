Limitations and scope

This application is intentionally scoped to experimental delivery and data collection. It does not replace theoretical design work, ethical review, or substantive statistical analysis.

In particular:

The app assumes that vignette designs, blocking decisions, and confounding structures are specified prior to data collection (e.g. in R). It does not optimise or infer designs automatically.
The application does not collect identifying personal data by default. GDPR compliance therefore depends on how researchers integrate the app into their broader data-collection workflow.
The app is designed for text-based vignette experiments. While other media formats could be added, these are outside the current scope.
The responsibility for ethical approval, informed consent, and appropriate use remains with the researcher.

The software is best understood as research infrastructure, not a methodological substitute.
