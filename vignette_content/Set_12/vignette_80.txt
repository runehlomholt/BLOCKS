Example vignette text

It is early in the morning, and Alex is getting ready for the day.

Before beginning work, Alex decides to make a cup of coffee.

The familiar morning routine feels calm and unhurried.

Alex chooses a mild, light-roasted coffee.

The coffee is poured into a small white cup.

Alex adds a small amount of milk to the coffee.

Alex sits down at the kitchen table.

Soft music plays while the coffee cools slightly.

The first sip is still a little hot, so Alex waits before trying again.
