Example vignette text

It is early in the morning, and Alex is getting ready for the day.

Before beginning work, Alex decides to make a cup of coffee.

The familiar morning routine feels calm and unhurried.

Alex chooses a rich, dark-roasted coffee.

The coffee is poured into a large blue mug.

Alex adds a small amount of milk to the coffee.

Alex stands for a moment beside the kitchen window.

The room is quiet while the coffee cools slightly.

After the first sip, Alex smiles and feels ready to start the day.
